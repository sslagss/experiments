First hour

  1. Orient. Tracks before the road: south coast. Road before the tracks: east coast.

  2. Move. Head north and inland. Skip Chernogorsk, Elektrozavodsk, Berezino.

  3. Drink. Fill a bottle at a well pump. Safe every time.

  4. Arm. Knife, or two small stones for a stone knife. Then a melee weapon.

  5. Carry. Backpack, or burlap sack + rope for a courier bag.

  6. Shelter. Before dark: matches, dry clothes, a place for a fire.

Actions

Start a fire
Tinder + stick → place fireplace → add sticks or firewood → light it

Light with matches, a hand drill or a flare. Hand drills can break on a failed try, so carry a spare. Build out of the rain. Fires glow at night, so build behind walls.

Cook
Meat on sharpened stick or pot on stand → over fire

Cook until it's no longer raw, and pull it before it burns. Raw or rotten food makes you sick.

Safe water
Well pump = safe · Pond → boil in pot or purification tablet
Hunt & gut
Kill → gut with knife → meat, fat, bones, pelt, guts

Wash your hands or wear gloves before eating.

Load & fire a gun
Ammo onto magazine → magazine onto gun → aim → fire

Ammo must match the caliber. Every shot carries far and pulls in infected and players.

Bandage
Rags or bandage → stand still until it finishes

Disinfect rags first to avoid infection.

Broken leg
Splint → walk, don't sprint

Crawl or move carefully until you can make one.

Threats
Players

Assume hostile. Keep distance. A downed player may only be unconscious, so make sure.

Infected

Crouch-walk through towns. Aim for the head. Fight in doorways.

Wolves

Packs, mostly inland and north. A howl means get indoors.

Bears

Two hits can drop you. Back away.

Toxic zones

Yellow fog or hazard signs: turn back without a full NBC suit.

Night

Pitch black on official servers. Your light gives you away.

Rain

Wet clothes freeze you. Raincoat, fire, shelter.

Safehouse
Where

Lone house or hamlet off main roads. Near a well, fruit trees and a pond.

Store

Tent, barrel or buried stash (shovel). Floor loot despawns. Revisit containers regularly.

Lock

Code lock, all maps since 1.30. Runs on a 9V battery and opens when it dies.

Build

Fence kits: nails, planks, hatchet, shovel.

Live

Garden (shovel + seeds), fishing, hunting, stone oven cooking.

Server

PvE or low-population community servers keep bases standing.

High-risk PvE

Heli crashes · follow the smoke. 10–15 high-tier items, military infected, no gas.

Toxic zones · static zones hold top loot. Dynamic zones: about 1:25 from artillery to death. 5-piece NBC suit, mask, filters.

Convoys · AI soldiers guard the wrecks.

Military bases · armored infected. One shot draws a crowd.

Bunkers · Dambog (Livonia), Sakhal shipwreck containers (key color must match), Nasdara underground.

Stash the good gear first. Raid light, bring loot home.
